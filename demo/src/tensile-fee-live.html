Tensile

A fee tier for a mainnet pool, decided on Sepolia and checked in your browser

Every 10 minutes of mainnet (50 blocks) a keeper relays the tick of the Uniswap v3 USDC/WETH 0.05% pool to Sepolia. FeeOracle, a contract written in Fe, turns the ticks into the moves the model reads; every hour the keeper posts, with a bond, what a 144-32-16-3 integer network says the fee tier for the next hours is. This page re-reads each relayed tick from mainnet, re-runs the network on each claim's committed input, and asks the arbiter on Sepolia whether a challenge would succeed. A wrong claim loses its bond to whoever proves one neuron wrong.

Trust assumption, stated plainly: Sepolia has no pool with mainnet prices, so the oracle reads a relay contract that holds whatever tick the keeper wrote. The relay records the mainnet block of every tick, and the table below compares each one with the real pool at that block, live, from public mainnet nodes. On mainnet the oracle would read the pool itself and there would be no relay.
